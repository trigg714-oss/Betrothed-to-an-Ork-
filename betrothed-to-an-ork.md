# Betrothed to an Ork!

The weight of your journey settles in your bones as you finally drop your pack. You are Ravok, a lone wanderer, and you have found a small, sheltered clearing to make camp for the night. The fading light of dusk filters through the trees. You are already battered and weary from a recent fight; You stormed a den of massive frost trolls single-handedly to avenge a village they had been terrorizing. You currently have a broken left arm, broken clavicle, countless flesh wounds ranging from bruises and scrapes up to deep gashes and bite wounds, your right leg is broken, the ankle dislocated. As the first stars appear, you've just managed to get a small fire crackling and your bedroll laid out when the silence is shattered.

From beyond the ridge comes the sounds of battle—the roar of a beast that shakes the very ground, the shouts of warriors, and the clash of steel on scale. You grab your sword and rush toward the noise without a second thought.

The scene in the small valley below is one of chaos and carnage. A band of orcs is locked in a desperate struggle against a massive black dragon. Several orc bodies already litter the ground. At the heart of the fray stands a scarred but beautiful orc woman, a shield on one arm and a wicked axe in the other, roaring defiantly at the monster. The dragon rears back, its chest glowing with hellish light, and unleashes a torrent of fire. The orc woman braces, her shield taking the full brunt of the blast. The force sends her to one knee, her shield arm smoking, but her will is unbroken as she fights to stand.

The dragon's head snakes forward for the killing bite. There is no time to think. You put all your strength into a desperate throw, sending your weapon spinning through the air to slam into the dragon's head, spoiling its aim. In the same motion, you charge forward, tackling the orc woman and sending you both tumbling just out of reach of its snapping jaws.

You scramble to your feet, finding her shield and axe where they fell. As the dragon shakes off its surprise and turns its smoldering gaze on you, you feel a surge of defiant courage. You charge, meeting its attack with a roar of your own. In a blur of motion, you bring the axe down in a savage arc across its snout. There is a crack, and a massive, obsidian tooth flies free, clattering to the ground. The dragon—Iediss the Dark—lets out a shriek of pain and fury before beating its vast wings and retreating into the darkening sky.

Silence returns, broken only by the crackle of nearby fires and your own ragged breaths. You turn as the orc woman rises to her feet. Her amber eyes take in the scene: the fleeing dragon, the fresh wounds on your body, and her own axe and shield in your hands. Her gaze finally rests on the dragon's tooth lying in the dirt. A realization clicks into place in your mind—you remember travelers' tales of ork customs, of brutal, combat-based proposals where saving a life with their own weapons is the highest form of courtship. The Shield's Vow.

She looks back to you, her expression shifting from one of a warrior assessing the field to something far more personal and intense. A slow, understanding nod is her answer.

"I accept," she says, her voice firm and final. You have not just saved her life; you have betrothed yourself to her.



"Accept what?" You mutter before your wounds take the best of you and you crumple to the ground.

Darkness. Then the slow, aching pull back to consciousness.

Your eyes crack open to the flickering light of torches against rough-hewn stone walls. The air smells of smoke, leather, and something hearty cooking. You're lying on a fur-covered cot, your broken limbs splinted and wrapped in clean bandages. A dull, throbbing ache radiates through your body, but you're alive.

The orc woman—Sharra—sits on a stool nearby, sharpening a knife with slow, deliberate strokes. Her amber eyes lift to meet yours the moment you stir.

"You're awake." She sets the knife aside, rising. "Took you three days. The shaman said your body was fighting hard to keep you." She crosses her arms, studying you.

"Da, you did," she replies, reaching for a clay mug beside your cot. "You also took a dragon tooth to its face. Not many can say that." She hands you the drink—something bitter and hot, steaming in the cool air.

You take it, your fingers brushing against hers for a moment longer than necessary. A quiet challenge hums between you. She's testing your reaction.

"And now?" You drink deeply, savoring the sharp bite of the brew.

Her gaze is steady, weighing you. "Now you have a choice." She gestures vaguely with one hand. "This is the Mazoga clan. I'm Sharra. You saved my life. That means you're mine now. At least, that's how we see it."
